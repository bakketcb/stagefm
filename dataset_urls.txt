Official sources for every external resource this project reads or names.

Every URL below was requested during the verification pass with a follow-redirects
client, answered with HTTP 200, and the returned document was checked to name the
resource on the line. A URL that could not be resolved, or whose document did not
name the resource, is omitted rather than guessed; the verification report records
which ones were probed (check env.dataset_links).

The private multi-centre clinical cohort (Sites A-E, Regions I-III) is not listed.
It is not distributed through any public repository, it is held under a data-usage
statement issued by the corresponding author, and it cannot be redistributed. The
manuscript's own data-availability statement says the same.

Access conditions. Merlin's weights and its paired CT-report dataset are released
under a data-use agreement and are gated on Hugging Face; the encoder is therefore
not bundled here and no clinical claim in this release depends on it. TotalSegmentator
and FLARE 2023 are openly downloadable, the former directly and the latter after
registering on the challenge page and signing its rule agreement. Neither public
resource contains gastric-cancer stage labels, and neither contributes any clinical
evidence to the endpoints reported in the manuscript.
