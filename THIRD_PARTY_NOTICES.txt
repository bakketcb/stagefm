Third-party material

This release is the authors' own implementation of the work described in "A
Multimodal Radiomics Foundation Model for Gastric Cancer Staging in Real-World
Multi-Center Imaging Cohorts". It bundles no third-party code, no third-party
weights and no third-party data. The resources below are named because the
implementation interoperates with them or because the manuscript's preprocessing
depends on them. Copyright in each remains with its authors.

Datasets and models

1. Abdominal CT vision-language foundation model and its paired CT-report dataset
   (the manuscript's Ref. [26]).
   Repository:   https://github.com/StanfordMIMI/Merlin
   Weights page: https://huggingface.co/stanfordmimi/Merlin
   Paper:        https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10181-8
   Licence:      distributed under a data-use agreement; access to the weights and
                 the paired dataset is gated and requires accepting that agreement.
                 Neither the weights nor the data are redistributed here. The
                 pipeline loads them only when a local checkpoint path is configured,
                 and records in its run metadata which encoder was used.
   Role:         the frozen image encoder whose attention projections carry the
                 low-rank adapters.

2. TotalSegmentator CT dataset (the manuscript's Ref. [59]).
   Repository:   https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator
   Dataset:      https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6802613
   Paper:        Wasserthal et al., Radiology: Artificial Intelligence 5, e230024 (2023).
   Licence:      the dataset is published under a Creative Commons Attribution
                 licence; consult the record's own terms before redistribution.
   Role:         reference organ definition for abdominal localisation in
                 preprocessing.

3. FLARE 2023 abdominal organ and pan-cancer segmentation challenge dataset (the
   manuscript's Ref. [60]).
   Repository:   https://github.com/JunMa11/FLARE
   Dataset:      https://zenodo.org/records/7844942
   Request page: https://codalab.lisn.upsaclay.fr/competitions/12239
   Paper:        Ma et al., arXiv:2408.12534 (2024).
   Licence:      distributed after registration on the challenge page and signature
                 of the challenge rule agreement; consult those terms before
                 redistribution.
   Role:         pan-cancer abdominal organ and lesion pretraining for the
                 localisation stage.

Software

The implementation depends on PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, statsmodels,
pandas and PyYAML, all under their own licences, and on pytest, ruff, black, isort
and mypy as development tooling. No source from those projects is copied into this
repository; they are consumed as installed packages.

Clinical cohort

The multi-centre gastric-cancer cohort (Sites A-E) is not third-party material and
is not redistributed. It is held under a data-usage statement issued by the
corresponding author. The records cannot be released at record level, and the
cohort-level clinical quantities reported in the manuscript are produced from the
real cohort only, never from the schema-compatible generator shipped here for
execution and verification purposes.
